## Intervention classification (per process step from 02)

| Step | Classification | Why | Failure consequence if wrong | Must remain human? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | eliminate · deterministic automation · conventional app/workflow · AI-assisted · agentic · human judgement | | | |

Classification guide: **eliminate** — the step exists only because of an upstream problem · **deterministic** — lookup, threshold, write, receipt · **conventional app/workflow** — structured input, routing, approval · **AI-assisted** — a person decides, AI drafts/summarises/suggests · **agentic** — AI interprets language/ambiguity and acts within delegated authority · **human** — accountability, commercial, policy, low-confidence.

Claude's role here is to challenge every *agentic* row: what would a deterministic version look like, and why is it insufficient?
