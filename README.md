# Architect

Warwick's AI solution architecture learning-and-delivery programme. Real, production-shaped AI products for PMO/implementation work are built here while deliberately developing and evidencing the skills of an **AI solutions architect** — not merely an AI-assisted builder.

Four frameworks are woven into one programme, interleaved by project need rather than studied in sequence:

| Layer | Question it answers | Exam plan |
|---|---|---|
| **PMI-CPMAI** | Should we do this, why, with what data, how is value measured? | Take (first) |
| **AB-100** | What should the overall solution be, and why? | Take (last) |
| **AB-410** | How do data, apps, flows and deterministic automation work? | Learn fully; exam optional |
| **AB-620** | How do agents reason, retrieve knowledge and safely use tools? | Take |

## Operating principles

1. Business outcome before technology.
2. Architecture before implementation — but architecture is provisional and evolves with evidence.
3. Authoritative business systems remain authoritative.
4. Deterministic where deterministic is enough; agentic where language, ambiguity or judgement adds value.
5. AI authority is explicit and risk-based; human review is risk-based, not ceremonial.
6. Claude accelerates but must not hide the concepts Warwick is meant to learn.
7. Every significant piece of work maps to syllabus objectives; every material architecture change produces an ADR.
8. Evidence what was *learned*, not what was generated.
9. Official/primary documentation is the source of truth; versions and retrieval dates are recorded.
10. Private evidence and public course material stay separate.

## Definition of success

Useful products shipped with measurable value · Warwick can explain and defend the architecture · objectives carry practical evidence · CPMAI, AB-620 and AB-100 readiness · portfolio-quality case studies · reusable, sanitised teaching material.
