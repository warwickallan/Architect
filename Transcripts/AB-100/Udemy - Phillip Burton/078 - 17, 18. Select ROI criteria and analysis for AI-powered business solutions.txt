[00:00] In this video we're going to have a look at possible return on investment ROI criteria for your AI powered business solutions and how you can design an analysis. You can see on screen the Microsoft Commission study business opportunity of AI created in 2025. It was undertaken by IDC and if I scroll down to page 28 you can see here the estimated return on investment for every one dollar spent on generative AI projects or initiatives. The average return is around according to this report three times the amount of the investment and 62 percent of companies reported an estimated two or three times the investment. So how do you calculate return on investment or ROI? Well there are two considerations tangible benefits and non-tangible or intangible benefits. So first of all tangible benefits which are more measurable. They include cost savings from lower labor costs and increased efficiency, revenue increase with new revenue streams such as subscription fees, productivity gains with enhanced tools for employees and freeing people up from just doing drudge work, things that could be automated, data quality improvements, reducing data entry and analysis errors, improvement of customer satisfaction with customer personalized experiences and a faster time to market with quicker production creation.

[01:41] Non-tangible or intangible benefits include improved decision making due to more comprehensive analysis, enhanced brand reputation if your services have indeed improved, increased employee satisfaction by automating boring tasks, increased compliance and increased innovation giving people more time to innovate. So how can you measure ROI? It is difficult. Metrics that you can measure include the quality of output, user satisfaction and engagement, width of impact, how much impact there has been including at the number of users and time to completion. What are some things you should avoid when calculating return on investment? Well you should avoid calculating it based on a single point of time as benefits may accrue longer term. So it isn't I've introduced AI where are all of my benefits? You might need to do it over a longer time period and you should avoid treating each AI project by itself in isolation. It is much more difficult to calculate the benefits and return on investment of a single AI project. You should treat it more in the whole all of the AI projects. So in this video we've had a look at how we could select ROI criteria and some considerations on how to create a return on investment analysis for an AI powered business solution.
