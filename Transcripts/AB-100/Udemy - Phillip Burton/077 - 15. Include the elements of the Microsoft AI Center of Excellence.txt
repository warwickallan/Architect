[00:00] In the previous video we had a look at the cloud adoption framework for Azure and the first methodology or phase is strategy and I said you should define your strategy team. This can also be known as an AI center of excellence so you can use this to ensure consistent AI adoption across your company and that AI solutions meet those responsible AI standards. So what does a AI center of excellence do? Well it can define AI strategy which aligns with business goals, build development programs, lead initial projects, establish AI standards and measure and report outcomes. So who should be on this team? Well it should include a C-level official, so like a CEO or CIO or a chief artificial intelligence officer. This gives executive sponsorship. There should be a head of AI, an AI leader and you should have a team consisting of AI roles. So some of them you will seek input from and others will be in your strategy team or the AI center of excellence. So this can include data scientists, machine learning engineers, AI architects, natural language prompting experts, prompt or AI agent engineers, AI security engineers and IT professionals who manage AI operations. So in this video we've had a look at how you can expand your strategy team to become an AI center of excellence to ensure consistent AI adoption across your company and that the AI solutions meet responsible AI standards.
