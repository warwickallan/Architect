## Verdict

The course is strong on the CPMAI lifecycle spine - the six phases, the PM's role and deliverables in each, and especially the trustworthy-AI principles (ethical/responsible/transparent/governed/explainable) embedded phase by phase - plus useful exam-technique walkthroughs and a plain-language ML/algorithm primer. It is thin on the Domain 2 and Domain 5 business and delivery mechanics the 2025 ECO weights heavily: scope statements, business case and ROI/TCO calculation, resource and procurement planning, adoption/change management, deployment planning and execution, transition/handover, and lessons-learned - all of which are named as deliverables but never actually taught. Treat it as a supplement and orientation layer over the mandatory PMI prep course and the AI guide, not as the spine; it maps the terrain and tells you where to focus, but will not by itself get you through the 26%+26% business and data domains at exam depth.
